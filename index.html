Dutch Blitz

Race three rivals to clear your Blitz pile — as fast as your hands allow.

Vanilla HTML · CSS · JavaScript — no backend, GitHub Pages ready

How to Play

The goal

Be the first player to clear all 10 cards from your Blitz pile. When you do, shout “Blitz!” and the round ends immediately.

Your setup

  • Blitz pile — 10 cards; only the top card is face up and playable.
  • Post piles — three single cards beside it, built down in alternating boy/girl colours.
  • Wood pile — the rest of your 40-card deck, turned over three cards at a time. Only the top card can be played.

During the round — everyone plays at once

  • Dutch piles (centre): four shared piles, one per colour, built 1 → 10 in the same colour. Any player may add to them.
  • Play the top card of your Blitz pile, a Post pile or your Wood pile onto a matching Dutch pile.
  • Build down your own Post piles — one lower, and the colour must switch between boys (blue & red) and girls (green & yellow).
  • Stuck? Turn your Wood pile over. When it runs out, the turned cards are gathered up and turned again.

Scoring

  • +1 for every card of yours that reached the Dutch piles.
  • −2 for every card still stuck in your Blitz pile.
  • First player to 75 points wins the game.

Controls

  • Click a card — plays it to the best legal spot (Dutch pile first).
  • Click your Wood pile or press Space — turn over the next cards.
  • Esc — pause the race.
